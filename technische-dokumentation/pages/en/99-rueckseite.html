A Computer
You Build Yourself

The HomeComputer 6502 is a home-built computer with the processor of the early eighties. Display, keyboard, sound chip and battery are built in.

This manual shows step by step how it comes to life: from the first clock signal to the finished device, with schematics, drawings of the builds and measurements.

Processor
MOS 6502, 1 MHz
Memory
32 KB RAM, 32 KB ROM
Display
LCD, 4 × 40 characters
Sound
SID 8580
