HomeComputer 6502 – Technical Documentation and Build Guide
Edition 1 (work in progress), September 2026

This manual describes step by step how the HomeComputer 6502 comes to life, and documents its circuitry along the way. Schematics and drawings have been carefully checked. Even so, errors can never be ruled out entirely. Hardware and software continue to be developed, so details may differ.

Building the computer is done at your own risk. No liability is accepted for any damage that may result.
