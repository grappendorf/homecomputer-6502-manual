CMemory Map

RAM runs from $0000 to $7EFF. The zero page and the stack are at the bottom, followed by the system variables, then your BASIC program. Starting at $7F00 are the chips for the interface, keyboard, display, and sound. From $8000 upward lies permanent storage with BASIC, Kernal, and Monitor. The six bytes at the very top are the jump vectors for NMI, Reset, and IRQ.
