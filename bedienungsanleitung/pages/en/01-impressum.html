HomeComputer 6502 – User Manual
Edition 1, September 2026

This manual describes the firmware with the startup message 6502 HOMECOMPUTER BASIC V1.1. All specifications and example programs have been carefully verified on the device and in the emulator. Even so, errors can never be ruled out entirely. Hardware and firmware continue to be developed, so details may differ from this manual.

Building and using the computer is done at your own risk. No liability is accepted for any damage that may result.
