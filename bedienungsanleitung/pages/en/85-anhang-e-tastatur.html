EKeyboard Layout

KEY(0) returns the code of the key that is currently pressed, or 0. The keyboard produces the same codes while you type. Caps Lock only swaps A to Z with a to z. Shift undoes that again.

Ä, Ö, Ü and ß as well as Shift+3 do not go into the input line. KEY still returns their codes. The matching characters are listed in Appendix D.
